🧪 Specimen Workflow

EXP provides mobile collection and transport. Specimens are delivered to the designated receiving laboratory for processing/testing under that laboratory's procedures.

Laboratory testing fees are separate unless specifically included in a written agreement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a lab order?

When requested testing requires an order or requisition, the patient is responsible for having the required documentation available.

Does the EXP price include laboratory testing?

No. EXP's service fee covers mobile collection/transport. Laboratory testing fees are separate unless specifically included in a written agreement.

How far does EXP travel?

Our local service area is 0–10 miles from the service base with extended-distance fees beyond that area.

Can EXP come to a facility?

Yes. Facility and recurring group services are available with customized pricing.

What if I need a same-day appointment?

Fast Track EXP is available for same-day appointments when scheduling allows.
